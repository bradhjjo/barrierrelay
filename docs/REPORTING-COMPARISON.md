## What BarrierRelay adds

WCAG success criteria, root-cause suggestions, before/after repair snippets and verification checklists: [apps/web/src/owner/issueExport.ts:155–159](../apps/web/src/owner/issueExport.ts#L155-L159). Optional keyboard trails: [apps/web/src/privacy/redact.ts:40–43](../apps/web/src/privacy/redact.ts#L40-L43). Scoped re-capture/re-check: [apps/web/src/ui/App.tsx:81–95](../apps/web/src/ui/App.tsx#L81-L95). These are suggestions and scoped client checks, not validated patches or WCAG certification. Approval-based sharing excludes raw notes and screenshots ([apps/web/src/privacy/redact.ts:17–18](../apps/web/src/privacy/redact.ts#L17-L18)); owner-link/private-token sanitization remains applied to issue exports ([apps/web/src/owner/issueExport.ts:73–135](../apps/web/src/owner/issueExport.ts#L73-L135)).

## Summary

8 of 10 items have coverage: 6 auto-filled and 2 reporter-filled with help; 5 of those 8 remain partial/conditional (URL, OS, browser, settings and assistive technology). The other 2 are omitted by privacy design: screenshot sharing and contact collection. This is an implementation comparison, not evidence of improved report quality or usability; no user study has been conducted.
